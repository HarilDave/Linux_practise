 1. Why is alloc_chrdev_region() better than register_chrdev()?

register_chrdev() uses a fixed major number → may conflict with other drivers.
alloc_chrdev_region() gives a free major dynamically → no conflict.
Dynamic major numbers are the modern, recommended method.
Works smoothly with udev + automatic /dev creation.



 2. What does class_create() actually do?

It creates a class entry in /sys/class/<your_class>/.
This class groups similar devices (e.g., all USB devices under “usb”).
It is required so that device_create() can place your device under that class.
Without class_create(), udev cannot know the device type.
